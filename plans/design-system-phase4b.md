# Design system Phase 4b: quotes on the brief page

## Goal / context
Migrate the quotes area of the brief page from the old tokens (`paper`, `ink`, `line`, `pink`, `blue`, `coverage`, `font-display`, `font-body`, `font-extrabold`) to the new design system: the Quote card (serif `text-message` text plus the shared `Author` block), the carousel and its empty state, media posts, source icons and attribution, the like, copy and source-link buttons, the add, detail and edit quote modals on the shared `Modal`, and the clarity-check flag panel (rose-wash flag panel or an `Alert`). Category chips are neutral with icons (`CategoryChip`). Keep all server actions, the clarity gating and the edit permissions exactly as they are. Source of truth: `docs/design/design-system/design-system.md` (2.2 Typography, 2.6 Iconography, 3.4 Identity, 3.5 Data display, 3.6 Feedback, 3.7 Overlays), `docs/design/design-system/migration.md` (token map) and `docs/design/design-system/migration-prompts.md` ("Standing rules" and the "4b" prompt). Tokens live in `app/app-theme.css`, shared primitives in `components/ui` (`Modal`, `ConfirmDialog`, `Alert`, `Author`, `CategoryChip`, `Tag`, `Avatar`, `Carousel`, `EmptyState`, `Field`, `ChoiceChips`, `Thumb`, `Skeleton`, `Toast`, `Menu`).

Plan 3 of 5 for the brief page (4a shell is merged to `master`; later plans: 4c Q&A and contributions, 4d sources, timeline, coverage, 4e remaining modals and brief editing). Branch from a fresh `origin/master`.

Decisions already made (`migration-prompts.md` "Decisions" and the 4a plan): status chips and category chips are neutral outside admin; brief reading font is serif; apply flow unchanged. Decisions made for this plan:
- A shared component is migrated fully by the step that owns it, and its callers in other areas accept the new look over an old surrounding component (the same trade-off accepted in 4a for `SectionHeader`). Owners: `LikeButton`, `CopyButton`, `SourceLinkButton`, `QuoteAuthorFooter` (step 2), `ClarityFlagsPanel` (step 4).
- Modals move onto the shared `Modal` (focus trap, Escape, restore focus come from it). Where a modal has a destructive action, use `ConfirmDialog` only if the action already asks for confirmation today; do not add new confirmations (behaviour change).
- Clarity flags render as an `Alert` with tone `warning` if its `lead`/`children` API fits the current content; otherwise a local rose-wash panel built from tokens (`bg-rose-wash`, `text-rose`). Decide in step 4 and log it.

Non-goals: no logic, data fetching, route, copy or behaviour changes (note bugs in the final step's Done line instead of fixing them); Q&A (`qa*.tsx`, `faq*.tsx`), votes outside quotes, endorse, contributions, feedback, sources, timeline, coverage bodies, related briefs, comments, contentious points, `components/PostModal.tsx` and the other modals (`brief-modals.tsx`, `ContributeModals`, `ReviewersModal`) stay on the old tokens for later plans; `app/admin` stays old; do not touch `<body>` styles; the app header, nav and explainer migrated in 4a are not touched. `lib/` and `supabase/` and every server action are untouched.
