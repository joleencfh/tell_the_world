Why this matters now

Attention shows up, understanding doesn't always follow.

AI incidents are all over the news and existential risk is reaching the mainstream. Now that people are finally paying attention, we need a way to keep the momentum going, while providing candid & credible explanations from the people and organisations who actually study this. We need to do this at scale, now.

C. The two demos as designed components

Both are short loops inside a framed window, in the same visual language as the page. The window is described once to assistive technology and the animated interior is hidden from it. A Pause button sits in the window chrome (and the loops start only when the window is mostly in view and stop when it leaves). With reduced motion set, each demo shows a still: the Q&A shows the full thread, and the quote demo shows the quote with its term flagged and the plainer wording suggested (the page mock above shows exactly these stills). The Q&A comments beat is removed because that feature does not exist yet. In the Q&A, answers are indented under the question, sit on a faint blue card, and are labelled, so an answer can never be mistaken for a question.
