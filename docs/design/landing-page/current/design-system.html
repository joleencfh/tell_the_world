Landing page design system

Version 0.1. Everything the finished landing page uses, and nothing else. Token names match landing-theme.css (Tailwind 4 @theme); the written rules are in design-system.md. Forced states here (hover, focus, error) are drawn statically so they can be compared side by side.

Colour

Rose is creators & journalists, cobalt is researchers & organisations. The three "graphic only" colours never carry text. Tints are tuned by hand to about 25% strength while keeping their hue.

Typography

Serif for anything read, Inter for controls, mono for short uppercase metadata. Sizes shown at desktop. Display and headings use clamp() so they shrink on phones.

Spacing, shape, elevation

Spacing (4px base)

Page gutter 40px (20px on mobile). Section padding 88px (56px on mobile). Content width 1080px. Touch targets 44px.
